## Findings, ranked

### M-1 — Decorative accent orb in the metric panel (`R-13`)
`components/work.tsx:216`

```tsx
<div aria-hidden="true" className="absolute -right-8 -top-8 h-40 w-40 rounded-full bg-accent/10 blur-3xl" />
```

It is the one element on the page that could have come from any AI landing page:
a blurred accent circle with no relationship to the grid plane it sits on.
**Recommendation**: replace it with a panel-local `radial-gradient` whose centre
tracks the pointer already used by `TiltPanel` (the same signal that drives the
glare), so the light has a physical cause — or remove it. Both keep the diorama
and drop the tell.

### M-2 — Em dash as a sentence break in the meta description (`copy`)
`lib/content/en.ts:29`, `lib/content/id.ts:30`

`"Portfolio of Ferdinand Dero Saputra — frontend engineer focused on …"` is the
sentence-interrupting usage the rule targets; it is also the line search engines
and share cards quote. **Recommendation**: `"Portfolio of Ferdinand Dero Saputra,
a frontend engineer focused on …"` (and the Indonesian equivalent). The section
markers `01 —` are a numbering device and are explicitly exempted in `DESIGN.md`.

### L-1 — Header and CTA targets below the 44px lock (`R-35`)
Measured at 390px / 1440px:

| Control | Rendered | Lock |
|---|---|---|
| Primary / ghost CTA | 189.5 × **43** | 44 |
| Language switch | 34.6 × **32** | 44 |
| Menu button | **32 × 32** | 44 |
| Desktop nav link | 59 × **31.5** | 44 |

**Recommendation**: expand the *hit area* without changing the visual size — an
`::after` inset of `-6px` on the icon controls and `min-height: 44px` on `.btn`.
Keeps the dense Linear-style header intact while meeting the lock. Verify with the
same `getBoundingClientRect` probe plus the axe suite.
